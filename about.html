OUR PROJECT

FODE Offline Learning Hub

The FODE Offline Learning Hub is an educational platform designed to make learning resources easier to access and organise.

The platform provides students with access to grades, subjects, units and PDF learning resources through a simple and user-friendly interface.

It is designed to work offline so that students can continue accessing their learning resources even when internet access is limited.

Our Goal

To provide students with an organised and accessible learning environment where educational resources can be easily found and used.

💡

Our Purpose

To make learning resources easier to access, especially for students who may have limited or unreliable internet connectivity.

📚

What We Provide

Grades, subjects, units, PDF learning resources, study tools, favourites and recently opened resources.
